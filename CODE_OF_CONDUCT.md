# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in SkillTape welcoming, respectful,
and safe for everyone. We welcome contributions from people with different
backgrounds, identities, abilities, and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and behavior without attacking people.
- Give and receive feedback in good faith.
- Protect private information and follow the project's security guidance.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual
attention, doxxing, publishing private information, and other conduct that
would be inappropriate in a professional setting are not acceptable. Deliberate
disruption of discussions, projects, or review processes is also unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone represents
SkillTape or its community. Maintainers may remove or edit contributions,
restrict participation, or take other appropriate action in response to
unacceptable behavior. They will handle reports as fairly and confidentially
as practical.

## Reporting

For a confidential conduct report, contact the repository owner through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/Chumaniac). Do not post confidential
details in a public Issue. Security vulnerabilities must follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
